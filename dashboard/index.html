How the score works, and change the weights

The score is a weighted average of the criteria below; weights are saved with your deal box. A criterion that can't be worked out (for example no SDE disclosed) is left out and the others re-weighted; those scores carry an asterisk. A price / SDE under 0.5× is treated as a likely data error and not scored on price or debt coverage.

In the table, "Benchmark" is what the industry sells for (times the local price level in the local view), and "vs benchmark" compares the asking multiple, reduced by the industry's usual sale-to-asking discount, with it. Green: at least 10% below; amber: at least 10% above. Click a row to open it in "Check a deal" with its score breakdown.
