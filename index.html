How Next Keepers works

From raw listing to ranked deal, automatically.

STEP 1

Collect

A nationwide, always-updating database of businesses for sale — plus off-market owners our team reaches by phone and in person.

STEP 2

Clean

Every listing normalized: SDE vs. EBITDA, real estate and FF&E separated, lease cost, full- and part-time staff, reason for sale, financing signals.

STEP 3

Score

Set your buy box once. Next Keepers ranks each deal on fit, valuation ratios and the local market around it.

STEP 4

Close

An AI-plus-human valuation, diligence support, and introductions to owners and co-investors when a deal is real.

Coming soon: the Next Keepers browser extension — open any listing, click once, and it's analyzed and saved to your pipeline.
