Privacy Policy

What Next Keepers collects, why, who helps us handle it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated October 20, 2026

The short version

  • If you join the waitlist we collect your email address and the role you pick (buyer, business owner, investor or broker). That is all.
  • We use it to tell you when Next Keepers opens and to share early-access news. We do not sell it.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • You can ask us to remove you at any time (see Contact).

Who we are

Next Keepers ("we", "us") is a deal-sourcing project for acquisition entrepreneurs, based in Buffalo, New York. This policy covers this website, including the market dashboard.

What we collect, and why

We do not collect payment details, government IDs or account passwords. There are no user accounts on this site.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set cookies and we do not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking tools.

Who handles your information

We use a small number of service providers, who handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • GitHub hosts this website. When you load a page, GitHub receives the technical details your browser sends.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser asks Google for the font files, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • A form service receives what you type into the waitlist form and stores it for us.
  • An email service may be used when we write to the people on the waitlist.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose it if the law requires. Our providers are mainly based in the United States, so your information may be handled there.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist details until you ask us to delete them or unsubscribe, and we review the list at least once a year and remove people who no longer wish to hear from us.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe. Every email we send will include a way to stop receiving them.
  • Access, correction and deletion. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent. You joined the list by choice and can leave at any time without giving a reason.
  • Your local rights. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the European Economic Area and United Kingdom) you may have additional rights under local privacy law. We will honor them. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Security

We keep waitlist details only with the providers described above and limit who can see them. No online service is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.

Children

Next Keepers is for adults who buy, sell or invest in businesses. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use the details of people already on the waitlist, we will tell them by email.

Contact

To ask a question, request access, or ask us to delete your information, message us on LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/yousef-taghizadeh. Please include the email address you used to sign up so we can find it.
